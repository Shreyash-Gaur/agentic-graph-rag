# 🧠 Welcome to the Agentic Graph RAG

Hello! I am your advanced AI assistant, powered by a semantic graph database and large language models. I'm here to help you analyze documents, explore connections, and answer complex questions.

### 🌟 What I can do for you:
* **Ingest Knowledge:** Upload documents and I will automatically break them down and map them into our Knowledge Graph.
* **Deep Reasoning:** Ask multi-hop questions, and I will navigate the graph to find connected insights.
* **Contextual Memory:** I remember our past conversations so we can pick up right where we left off.

### 🚀 How to get started:
1. 📎 **Upload a file:** Click the attachment icon in the chat bar below to add a document to my knowledge base.
2. 💬 **Ask a question:** Type a prompt like *"Summarize the key entities in the uploaded document"* to begin!
